About Us

Miracle Hotel & Restaurant is a family-run boutique hotel located on the shores of Phewa Lake in Pokhara, Nepal. Since 2012 we have welcomed travelers from around the world with warm Nepali hospitality, comfortable rooms and authentic cuisine.

Our 28 rooms range from cozy standard rooms to spacious suites with private balconies overlooking the lake and the Annapurna range. The restaurant serves traditional Nepali dal-bhat, momos, Chinese and Indian dishes, plus continental breakfast.
